Whose label these come from

The gaps below were found by auditing an aggregating atlas: hECA v2.0 ingests published datasets and rewrites their annotations into a single vocabulary, so the label under test belongs to that harmonisation step, not to the people who generated the cells. A gap here therefore means the atlas as distributed names a population CL has no term for — it is not a claim that any depositing author was wrong. Where the audit finds a mismatch rather than a gap, the atlas’s own original_name field frequently agrees with the original authors and not with the harmonised label. Proposals drawn from a primary, natively CL-annotated atlas (Tabula Sapiens) or from individual CELLxGENE datasets are labelled as such where they appear.
